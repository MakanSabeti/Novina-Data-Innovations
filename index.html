How it works

Drone orthomosaic footprints as GeoJSON or LiDAR-derived elevation grids. A single raw photo works too.

Exact percent complete

Image recognition and polygon overlap measure footprint completion. Volumetric cut/fill and stockpile tonnage are computed alongside.

AI Powered Schedule Forecasting

Flags before they're expensive

Platform
Detailed Analysis from a photo

Upload a planned-site GeoJSON and an as-built capture, and Novina computes completion with 99% accuracy.

Computer vision reads grading and structures straight from the imagery.

Forecasts that follow the real shape of construction progress

Progress isn't linear. Slow mobilization gives way to a fast middle before the slow punch-list finish.

Novina fits a logistic S-curve across your full capture history instead of drawing a straight line, and it flags stalled features along with schedule variance.

Works with your stack

GeoJSON, LiDAR / DEM, Drone Orthomosaics, Claude Vision API, Python Flask, REST API, Slack, SMTP / Email, SQLite / SQL, ArcGIS, Pix4D / DroneDeploy and Propeller Aero.

About
Built by the person who used to do this by hand.

Novina Data Innovations was founded on a simple premise: mining and infrastructure teams aren't short on site data, they're short on a reliable way to turn that data into a decision.

Before Novina, Makan designed geospatial platforms and LiDAR recognition systems at Hatch that eliminated manual re-scans and hours of labor. That work showed him the gap Novina now fills.
