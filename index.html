How it works

Four steps from raw capture to a number leadership trusts.

The same pipeline runs whether it's your first site or your fiftieth.

01 — CAPTURE

Upload what you already fly

Drone orthomosaic footprints as GeoJSON, LiDAR-derived elevation grids, or a single raw photo. Novina is vendor-neutral; it reads whatever your existing drone program or capture partner already produces.

02 — MEASURE

Exact percent complete

Image recognition and polygon overlap for footprint completion, volumetric cut/fill and stockpile tonnage computed, and cross-checked against hand-calculated ground truth before it ships.

03 — FORECAST

AI Powered Schedule Forecasting

A logistic growth model fit across your full capture history projects a realistic completion date. One that accounts for the slow start and slow finish real construction progress actually has.

04 — ALERT

Flags before they're expensive

Boundary encroachment, standing water, pond capacity, and schedule slippage are checked automatically and pushed to Slack or email at whatever threshold you set.

Novina Data Innovations was founded on a simple premise: mining and infrastructure teams aren't short on site data, they're short on a reliable way to turn that data into a decision.

As a Solution Architect at Hatch, I designed geospatial data platforms for real-time risk monitoring and built LiDAR-based recognition systems that auto-identified degraded industrial instruments, eliminating costly re-scans and thousands of labor hours. The pattern showed up everywhere: teams were already capturing the data they needed, but nobody had built the layer that turned a point cloud into a percent-complete a project director could trust.

That's the layer Novina builds. Not a drone service, not another dashboard, but the analysis engine that sits between your capture pipeline and the decision it's supposed to inform: exact completion percentages, volumetric earthwork, S-curve schedule forecasting, and automated risk flags, built with the same rigor as the geospatial and predictive systems I shipped at Hatch.

Novina exists for teams that want that rigor applied to their own sites, without becoming a drone operator or hiring a GIS specialist to get it.
